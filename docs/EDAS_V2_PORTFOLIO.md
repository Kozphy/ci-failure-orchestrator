## Evidence-backed project descriptions

**SWE / CI Platform / AI Reliability.** Audited a pinned Python CI-orchestration snapshot and
ten recent commits, reproduced false-green evaluation and contradictory verification-predicate
defects, and added targeted fail-closed checks. Captured before/after outputs and preserved an
existing 38-case synthetic benchmark without changing its expectations or baseline.

**Technology Risk / IT Audit / AI Governance / Data Governance.** Built a synthetic advisory
reference with provenance hashes, dependence groups, conflicting-evidence review, version-bound
human review, explicit unresolved outcomes and invalidation after new evidence. Published a
claim-to-test evidence table and a governance crosswalk with stated source/coverage limits.

**Financial Systems relevance.** Demonstrated transferable concepts: authorization distinct
from evidence validation, separation of proposer/reviewer IDs, bounded investigation budgets,
audit linkage and reconciliation of inconsistent flags. No transaction processing, financial
reporting assertion testing, regulatory validation or production payment control was performed.

## Research limitations statement

The author designed both policy and fixtures; expectations are not independent ground truth.
Cases are small, synthetic and deliberately selected; no incident distribution, real source
reliability, probability calibration, causal benefit or business outcome was measured. Logical
clock/cost inputs are supplied by the caller. No authenticated reviewer, durable EDAS storage,
real financial/medical/legal records, isolated untrusted-code executor, external replication
or formal proof is present. The full local pytest/lint/type suite could not run because tools
were absent and shell network access failed. The published implementation later passed the
full GitHub CI workflow; this validates the recorded implementation SHA in one CI environment,
not independent scientific replication or operational readiness. These limits must accompany
any interview or resume discussion.

## Next three highest-value engineering improvements

1. **Make independent reproduction easy.** Pin Python/dependencies/runner images, execute the
   unchanged full suite plus new tests in CI, and obtain a reviewer-owned fresh-clone run.
   This turns local targeted evidence into a reproducible engineering story.
2. **Enforce actual authority and trustworthy evidence boundaries.** Add authenticated reviewer
   roles, trusted acquisition, revision/policy digests, transactional durable state and externally
   anchored audit tips; test restart, corruption and replay. Keep external effects disabled
   until authorization, idempotency and reconciliation are demonstrated.
3. **Validate usefulness independently.** Preregister a hidden-case evaluation with a real
   checklist baseline and one-factor ablations, independently adjudicate labels, report losses,
   abstentions and denominators, and obtain external replication. Only then discuss research
   effectiveness or controlled operational pilots.
