## Private CRM minimum fields (store outside public repository)
Opaque pilot ID; date; buyer role; problem category; permission status; scopes/workflows/runs; quoted price; payment status (without card information); time spent; delivery date; refund status; client feedback consent; follow-up date. Never publish private account information.

## Financial definitions
- Revenue: cleared payments less refunds; **not** quoted pipeline value.
- Direct cost: documented delivery labor (hours × assigned rate) + tools and processing + per-order payment fees.
- Contribution per pilot: net revenue minus direct cost (before fixed costs, taxes, acquisition).
- Conversion: paid pilots / qualified leads, with numerator, denominator and time window.
- Verified client outcome: post-delivery measurable improvement with client approval; never substitute internal synthetic benchmarks.

## 30-day experimental targets (not forecasts)
30 relevant prospects contacted with context; 5 useful replies; 3 discovery calls; 1–3 paid pilots; at least one accepted report; seek explicit permission for one anonymized case study.

## Decision gate
After first 3 genuinely independent pilots, assess repeated pain, willingness to pay, labor hours, contribution, refund risk and whether a repeat engagement is requested. Do not describe product-market fit or recurring revenue from a single sale.
