# CI Reliability Audit — Proposed Pilot Service Terms

> **Draft for legal and merchant review, not an activated checkout agreement.** Before accepting payments, fill in the legal provider identity, contact details, applicable tax/invoice treatment, jurisdiction, mandatory consumer notices, secure payment provider and actual data-transfer channel. These placeholders are not public booking links.

## 1. Scope and price
US$149 one-time for a written, read-only assessment of **one** eligible GitHub repository, up to **five** named workflows and **thirty** recent runs where data is accessible. Deliverables: Markdown + JSON reports, up to five evidence-backed recommendations, one written clarification round. No auto-renewal. Disclose applicable fees/taxes before collection.

## 2. Order and schedule
An order is accepted only after both parties approve scope, secure information-sharing, delivery channel, these terms and payment. The delivery target is three business days after payment confirmation **and** receipt of usable authorized data. Business days are Monday–Friday excluding Taiwan public holidays. If intake is insufficient, contact client for a remedy, amended schedule or appropriate refund.

## 3. Authorization and confidentiality
Client warrants legal authorization to supply data for review. Client should provide sanitized exports rather than access credentials. No passwords, personal access tokens, secrets or raw confidential logs in GitHub issues, email or ordinary chat. Additional private read-only access requires separately agreed secure transfer, single-repo scope, minimum permissions, expiry and revocation. Provider must not transmit confidential code/logs to external AI vendors absent prior express consent, nor use customer information as public case-study material without written consent.

## 4. Work boundaries
No repository writes, CI changes, patch execution, production access, deployment, guaranteed error resolution, cybersecurity or compliance certification, continuous monitoring, or emergency support. Findings are based only on the supplied evidence and may contain uncertain classifications; implementation decisions belong to the client.

## 5. Delivery and acceptance
Provider delivers both report files through an agreed secure channel. The client may request one written clarification or fact correction within five business days, acknowledged within two business days. A material factual error will be corrected within a reasonable mutually agreed time. Different technical opinions alone do not automatically invalidate delivery. Silence never waives non-waivable statutory rights.

## 6. Refunds
Provide a full refund if the provider cannot begin due to a provider-side failure, cannot produce core deliverables and no workable correction exists, or determines before substantive work that a viable authorized audit is impossible. Materially defective reports receive correction or an appropriate refund. The provider may voluntarily refund a delivered audit with no meaningful actionable finding. Mandatory statutory rights and provider obligations always prevail. Refund using original method when available.

## 7. Intellectual property and retention
Client retains ownership of original code, repository, logs and data, and may use/adapt reports for internal business purposes after payment. Provider retains pre-existing code, templates and methodologies subject to applicable licenses. Access is revoked as soon as no longer needed. Proposed raw data retention: 14 calendar days after acceptance or closure of clarification period (whichever later), except legally required or separately agreed retention. Notify client without undue delay on discovery of relevant data breach.

## 8. Disputes and legally required disclosures
Parties first seek written resolution. Legal provider identity, contact, governing law, venue, taxes, invoice and applicable Taiwan/other consumer rights must be settled before publishing final terms or accepting a payment. Mandatory rights override any conflicting clause.
